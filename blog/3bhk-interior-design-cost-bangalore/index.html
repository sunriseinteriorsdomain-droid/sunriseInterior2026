How Much Does a 3BHK Home Interior Cost in Bangalore in 2026?

Review realistic 2026 budget tiers, room-wise allocations, materials and quote tips for your Bengaluru 3BHK.

Taking possession of a 3BHK apartment in Bengaluru—whether in Whitefield, Sarjapur Road, Yelahanka, or Kanakapura Road—is an exciting milestone. Immediately after handover, almost every homeowner asks: what is the realistic 3BHK interior design cost in Bangalore in 2026?

In 2026, the average 3BHK home interior cost Bangalore homeowners pay typically falls between ₹7.5 lakhs and ₹25 lakhs+, depending on apartment carpet area, storage volume, core plywood grade, and surface finishes. Quotations can differ by ₹6 to ₹8 lakhs based on finishes, hardware, ceilings, and other specifications.

Room-Wise 3BHK Flat Interior Design Cost Breakdown

1. Modular Kitchen: 22%–28% of Total Budget

The kitchen is typically the highest-cost zone per square foot. Include base cabinets, wall units, a tall pantry, loft storage, tandem drawers, corner carousels, and a quartz or granite counter. BWP marine plywood carcasses offer strong protection against humidity and sink moisture.

2. Master Bedroom and Two Secondary Bedrooms: 32%–38%

Across three bedrooms, storage creates the largest carpentry expense. Add full-height wardrobes, lofts, dressing units, upholstered beds with hydraulic storage, study desks, and compact bookshelves as required.

3. Living Room, Dining Area and Foyer: 18%–22%

Key costs include TV units, a foyer cabinet and seating, crockery storage, pooja units, and feature treatments such as fluted panels or stone cladding.

4. False Ceiling, Electrical and Painting: 12%–18%

Gypsum ceilings, cove lighting, profile strips, switchboards, concealed wiring, and wall painting tie the complete modern 3BHK interior together.

7 Key Factors That Decide the Interior Design Price for a 3BHK Flat

  • Carpet area and floor geometry: Larger layouts and extra balconies require more cabinetry and ceiling work.
  • Core board selection: BWP plywood costs more than MR plywood or MDF but performs better in wet zones.
  • Shutter finishes: Acrylic, PU and veneer can raise carpentry costs by 25%–45% over laminates.
  • Hardware and accessories: Tandem drawers, magic corners, pantries and lift mechanisms add cost and convenience.
  • Custom loose furniture: Built-in beds, dining tables, center tables and sofas increase the turnkey scope.
  • Ceiling and lighting complexity: Multi-level ceilings and magnetic track lights cost more than simple perimeter gypsum.
  • Civil, plumbing and electrical changes: Sink-point shifts, rewiring and wall changes are outside standard modular work.

Fresh Handover vs. Resale 3BHK Apartment: Hidden Costs

A new apartment usually has fresh plaster, flooring and standard electrical points, so budgets can focus on interiors. A resale flat may need dismantling, waterproofing, rewiring, damp repair, and fresh civil work. Set aside a 10%–15% contingency for a resale renovation.

How to Plan Affordable 3BHK Interiors in Bangalore Without Cutting Corners

  • Use BWP plywood in kitchens and utilities and HDHMR or MR boards in dry bedroom wardrobes.
  • Prioritize built-in storage over loose furniture.
  • Limit feature walls to two focal zones.
  • Complete dusty civil, electrical, ceilings, kitchen and wardrobes first if you need to execute in phases.

How to Compare Quotes from 3BHK Interior Designers in Bangalore

Never compare quotations only on the bottom-line total. Request an itemized BOQ and verify board brands and thickness, consistent measurement methods, GST and exclusions, warranties, delivery timelines, and payment milestones.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

  1. What is the average 3BHK interior design cost in Bangalore in 2026?
    Expect ₹7.5–₹11 lakhs for essential interiors, ₹11–₹17 lakhs for mid-range contemporary interiors, and ₹17–₹26 lakhs+ for luxury bespoke work.
  2. What is the per-square-foot interior design price for a 3BHK?
    Bengaluru turnkey rates commonly range from ₹650 to ₹1,600+ per sq. ft. of carpet area, with exact quotes based on actual cabinet size.
  3. How much does a modular kitchen cost in a 3BHK flat?
    Typically 22%–28% of the project budget—around ₹1.8–₹3.2 lakhs for laminates on BWP plywood and ₹3.5–₹6 lakhs+ with acrylic or PU, quartz and premium hardware.
  4. Can I get affordable 3BHK interiors under ₹10 lakhs?
    Yes. Prioritize the kitchen, three bedroom wardrobes with lofts, a compact TV unit, foyer storage, matte laminates, and selective ceiling work.
  5. Which room takes the largest share of a 3BHK budget?
    Bedrooms together take roughly 32%–38%; the modular kitchen is the largest individual room expense at 22%–28%.
  6. Which plywood is best for a 3BHK apartment?
    Use IS:710 certified BWP plywood in kitchens, utilities and bathroom vanities. Use MR plywood or HDHMR for dry bedroom and living-room areas.
  7. What is the difference between laminate, acrylic and PU finishes?
    Laminate is durable and cost-effective, acrylic gives a high-gloss mirror look, and PU offers seamless premium matte or gloss finishes.
  8. Does resale 3BHK design cost more than new-handover design?
    Usually 15%–25% more due to dismantling, rewiring, waterproofing, plastering and plumbing updates.
  9. How long does a 3BHK interior project take?
    After design and finishes are finalized, manufacturing and installation generally take 45–60 working days.
  10. Are false ceilings included in standard packages?
    Basic packages may include minimal work only; mid-range and premium packages often include gypsum ceilings and layered lighting.
  11. How can I reduce cost without compromising quality?
    Mix BWP and HDHMR where appropriate, use matte laminates in less visible areas, limit feature walls, and use built-in lofts.
  12. Do 3BHK designers charge a separate design fee?
    Many turnkey firms adjust or waive the initial design fee after project confirmation.
  13. What hidden costs should I check in a 3BHK quote?
    Check GST, civil changes, countertops, sink and hob cutouts, painting, deep cleaning, transportation, and society debris or security charges.
