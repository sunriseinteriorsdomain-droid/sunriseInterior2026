Pre-Interior Handover Checklist for Kanakapura Road Apartments

  1. Wall & Ceiling Dampness Check: Moisture-meter inspection of external walls, bathroom shared walls, and balcony thresholds before paneling.
  2. Electrical Load & Point Mapping: Verify sanctioned load and map appliance and ceiling-light wiring routes.
  3. Plumbing & Utility Slope Verification: Test wet-kitchen and utility drainage before installing base cabinets.
  4. Society NOC & Service Lift Scheduling: Coordinate builder MEP drawings, association permits, and service elevator slots.
